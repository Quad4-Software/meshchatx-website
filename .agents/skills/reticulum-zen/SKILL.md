# Zen of Reticulum

Canonical text: https://reticulum.network/manual/zen.html

Also in the Reticulum tree as `Zen of Reticulum.md`. Author: Mark Qvist.

This is a design-principles document, not a product feature list. Quote or paraphrase carefully. Prefer linking to the manual over inventing slogans.

## Points that matter for site copy

- There is no cloud. There are other people's computers. Building for a landlord means your app exists only by their permission and uptime.
- Reticulum is a fabric you inhabit, not a service you subscribe to.
- The goal is uncentralizability as a protocol property, not a marketing claim about goodwill.
- Peers and links are assumed potentially hostile. No privileged nodes that can see who talks to whom as a matter of design privilege.
- An address is a hash of an identity (who), not a place (where).

## Writing constraints

- Do not turn the Zen into dramatic movie-poster headings.
- Do not invent quotes from Mark Qvist. Link the manual or quote verified text only.
- Keep MeshChatX claims separate: MeshChatX is a client. The Zen describes Reticulum.
