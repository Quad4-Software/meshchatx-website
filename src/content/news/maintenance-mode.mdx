---
title: "Maintenance only mode, then end of life on 1 January 2028"
date: 2026-10-25
description: "MeshChatX moves to maintenance only mode after v5 for 12 months, then reaches end of life on 1 January 2028."
---

MeshChatX was going to reach end of life on 1 January 2027. I decided to move that date instead.

After the v5 release, MeshChatX will be in maintenance only mode for 12 months, then reach end of life on 1 January 2028.

During maintenance only mode:

- Bugs and security issues are the priority.
- No new features are planned.

I still advise everyone to find an alternate client.
